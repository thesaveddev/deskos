# Brag Plan: ReyDesk

## What is this app?
ReyDesk is a hosted IT support workspace that puts remote support, ticketing, endpoint health, and governed AI assistance in one technician console.

## The angle
A calm, specific “one incident, one console” walkthrough: a technician sees a device signal, opens the ticket, starts a consented remote session, and leaves an auditable resolution record. The video should show ReyDesk doing the work rather than reciting a feature list.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
“From alert to resolved — without changing tools.” Reveal the live-looking ticket queue immediately, then draw a clean path from the alert to the consented session.

## Key moments (the middle)
- An endpoint alert for high CPU appears beside an open support ticket.
- The technician opens the ticket and starts a one-time-code remote support session with explicit user consent.
- The same console shows the session, device context, and a clear audit trail after resolution.

## Outro / punchline
“Remote support, ITSM, and endpoint health. One ReyDesk workspace.” End on the ReyDesk wordmark and “Start your free workspace”.

## User flow worth showing
Queue → ticket context → consented remote session → resolved ticket with audit record.

## Tone
- Preset: polished
- Creative direction: restrained product walkthrough for a serious mid-market IT operations platform
- Interpretation: confident, legible, and tactile; use real ReyDesk labels and a small number of purposeful transitions instead of generic SaaS spectacle.

## Format: landscape — 1280x720
## Duration: 20 seconds

## Visual identity (from the project)
- Background: #0e1114
- Accent: #e8a33d
- Text: #e6e9ec
- Display font: IBM Plex Sans, 600 weight
- Body font: IBM Plex Sans with IBM Plex Mono for system labels
- Strongest visual element: the dark technician console with amber status accents, ticket rows, device signals, and consent language

## Share copy (draft)
ReyDesk takes support from the first device signal to a consented remote session and a clean audit trail — without making technicians change tools.

## Audio direction
- Role: sparse professional accents
- Music: restrained electronic bed with a low pulse
- Music treatment: quiet fade-in, small lift at the remote-session reveal, fade under the final wordmark
- Music cue guidance: detect at composition time; use strong cues for the ticket reveal and final logo landing
- Audio-reactive treatment: subtle; let the console glow and accent line breathe slightly with the bed
- SFX posture: sparse; short UI clicks for sequential rows and one restrained confirmation cue
- Audio-coupled moments: ticket row arrival, consent confirmation, final logo lockup
- Restraint rule: no notification spam, no synthetic hype, no voiceover

## Storyboard

### Scene 1 — The signal — 3s
A dark ReyDesk console appears. A device alert and ticket row arrive in sequence. Text: “From alert to resolved.” Sequential/interaction: yes — alert, ticket, and SLA badge appear one by one, then hold. Audio intent: establish focus and confidence. Audio-coupled idea: sparse click on each UI arrival. Transition mood: clean → Scene 2

### Scene 2 — The context — 4s
The ticket expands to show the affected device, recent signal, and technician action rail. Text: “The context stays with the ticket.” Sequential/interaction: yes — device context and action controls reveal in order. Audio intent: quiet forward motion. Audio-coupled idea: soft card movement accents. Transition mood: smooth slide → Scene 3

### Scene 3 — Consent first — 5s
A remote-support panel opens with a visible one-time code and permissions summary. A consent state changes from waiting to approved. Text: “Remote support starts with consent.” Sequential/interaction: yes — show the technician starting the session, then the endpoint approval state. Audio intent: one clear confirmation moment. Audio-coupled idea: subtle confirmation cue on approval. Transition mood: clean wipe → Scene 4

### Scene 4 — Leave the record — 4s
The session view collapses into the ticket. Resolution note, device status, and audit event appear together. Text: “Every action leaves a record.” Sequential/interaction: yes — resolution, audit event, and status arrive in that order and hold. Audio intent: settle the story. Audio-coupled idea: three restrained ticks. Transition mood: soft crossfade → Scene 5

### Scene 5 — ReyDesk — 4s
ReyDesk wordmark and product line on the dark console palette. Text: “Remote support · ITSM · endpoint health” and “Start your free workspace”. Sequential/interaction: none. Audio intent: confident landing. Audio-coupled idea: final logo accent, music fades. Transition mood: none

**Music mood for this video:** polished, restrained, quietly technical
**Audio summary:** A low, unobtrusive bed supports the workflow, with sparse UI accents for the actual product actions and a clean final landing.
